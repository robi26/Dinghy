A new icon, a navigation bar, and file previews.

* Dinghy has an icon of its own, including a themed one
* Devices, Folders, Search and Settings are separate tabs
* Search now covers every folder at once, and results open
* Preview images, text and PDFs without downloading them
* Follows the system dark theme
* Nothing hides behind the status bar or the gesture handle any more
* Folder browsing shows where you are and how to get back

32-bit ARM (armeabi-v7a) is no longer published.

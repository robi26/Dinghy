Back up your photos, without copying them anywhere.

* A photo folder syncs your photo library as it is, with no second copy on the phone
* Photos reach your other devices sorted by year and month, as you take them
* It mirrors the library: a photo you delete here is deleted on your other devices too
* Files copied into a folder from the file picker now actually reach your other devices
* Dinghy is offered as a destination when another app saves a file

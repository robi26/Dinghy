Devices and Folders are lists now.

* Devices shows your devices, with an icon for which are connected
* Tap a device for its details, or to remove it
* Folders shows one row per folder, with what it syncs and what it stores
* Add device and Add folder are buttons under each list, and return to it
* Folder sharing moved behind the folder, and names devices properly
* Start and Stop moved to Settings
* Connection state updates while you are looking at it
